Protocol briefing

How the exchange works

  1. Signaling randomly pairs two sockets and relays only WebRTC negotiation.
  2. An encrypted DataChannel opens directly when possible or through TURN when required.
  3. File metadata crosses the peer channel. Both people must accept before bytes move.
  4. Chunks are sequence-checked, written, and SHA-256 verified before completion.

Privacy and safety

No accounts, analytics, trackers, or server-side file storage exist. Signaling and TURN operators can still observe IP addresses, timing, and traffic volume. Your peer learns the filename, size, type, and contents.

Every peer and file is untrusted. ByteRoulette does not scan for malware, prove identity, guarantee anonymity, or guarantee a direct route.
